# PRODUCT.md — Aero Tech Labs

register: brand

## Product purpose
Aero Tech Labs is a contract aerosol filling facility in Fort Lauderdale, FL. It fills
customers' liquid and chemical products into pressurized aerosol cans, and supplies
medical/industrial-grade gas. Core offerings:
- 1-inch opening contract aerosol filling
- 20mm opening contract aerosol filling
- Non-flammable propellants (HFC-134a, HFO-1234ze)
- EnviroLase Laser Cryogen coolant: high-purity 1,1,1,3-Tetrafluoropropene for medical devices

The site runs e-commerce for the cryogen product (Stripe + Supabase) and a
consultation/quote funnel for filling services. Domain: customfiller.com.

## Users
B2B buyers: procurement, R&D, and operations staff at cosmetics, personal-care,
medical-device, and industrial-product companies. They are evaluating whether to trust
a contract filler with their product, or sourcing a reliable medical-grade coolant.
The decision rides on perceived precision, safety/compliance, and responsiveness.
These are not consumer shoppers.

## Voice (three physical words)
Precise (a calibrated pressure gauge). Clean and clinical (a medical-grade canister
label). Engineered (anodized aluminum, a spec sheet). Confident and plainspoken,
technically credible, no hype.

## Register
brand. The customer-facing site sells trust, so design is part of the product.
The internal `/admin` and `/dashboard` areas are product register and are a later phase.

## Anti-references
- Generic "industrial" steel-gray sites with hazard stripes.
- SaaS-template hero: gradient blob, one giant number, three identical icon cards.
- Editorial-magazine affectation (serif italic headlines + mono labels). Wrong register.
- Cluttered auto-playing video carousels.

## Strategic principles
- Make precision and safety legible at a glance.
- Fewer, calmer pages. One clear idea per fold.
- Preserve the existing, well-built SEO: metadata, structured data, sitemap, robots.
- Brand name is "Aero Tech Labs"; the domain stays customfiller.com.
